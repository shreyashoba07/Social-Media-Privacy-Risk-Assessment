# Defensive Threat Model

| Asset | Threat | Potential impact | Recommended control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account access | Phishing or account takeover | Loss of account control | MFA, unique password, login alerts |
| Contact information | Public phone/email exposure | Spam, impersonation, targeted scams | Hide unnecessary contact details |
| Location privacy | Real-time location or routine posts | Unwanted attention or physical safety risk | Avoid real-time location broadcasts |
| Social relationships | Impersonation of known contacts | Fraudulent requests | Verify through a separate trusted channel |
| User assessment answers | Excessive application data collection | Privacy loss from the assessment tool itself | Data minimization; do not persist answers |
| Connected apps | Unused or excessive permissions | Unnecessary data access | Review and revoke unneeded integrations |

This is a lightweight educational threat model. Actual likelihood and impact depend on context.
